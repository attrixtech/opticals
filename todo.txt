# MASTER DEVELOPMENT PROMPT
# OPTICAL STORE MANAGEMENT ERP
## Complete Optical Retail, POS, Prescription, Lens Laboratory, Inventory & Business Management Software
### Laravel + PHP + MySQL | Production-Ready Enterprise Application

---

# 1. PROJECT OVERVIEW

Act as a senior Laravel software architect, optical retail business consultant, optometry workflow specialist, inventory management expert, POS developer, database architect, and enterprise UI/UX designer.

Your task is to design and develop a complete, production-ready Optical Store Management ERP using Laravel, PHP, and MySQL.

This must be a complete business management system for optical stores, not simply a billing or inventory application.

The software must manage the complete optical retail lifecycle:

Customer Registration → Eye Prescription → Eye Measurements → Frame Selection → Lens Selection → Quotation → Order Creation → Lens Processing → Quality Control → Fitting → Final Billing → Delivery → Warranty → After-Sales Service.

The application should support:

1. Independent optical retail shops.
2. Multi-branch optical chains.
3. Spectacle stores.
4. Contact lens retailers.
5. Prescription eyewear businesses.
6. Sunglasses retailers.
7. Lens laboratories.
8. Optical wholesalers.
9. Optical distributors.
10. Optical manufacturers.
11. Optometry clinics attached to optical stores.
12. Online eyewear businesses.

Primary market: India.

Design the software to support Indian retail billing, GST configuration, barcode inventory, supplier management, customer prescriptions, and multi-branch operations.

# 40. IMPORTANT BUSINESS RULES

## 40.1 Prescription Integrity

- Store right-eye and left-eye values independently.
- Preserve prescription history.
- Maintain original values.
- Record who created and verified prescriptions.
- Do not silently overwrite prescription records.
- Validate units and supported formats.
- Require review of OCR-extracted data.

## 40.2 Sales Order Integrity

- Every prescription-based order must link to an appropriate prescription.
- Required measurements must be validated.
- Frame and lens specifications must be stored against the order.
- Confirmed order specifications must be versioned.
- Changes after laboratory submission must require authorization and a documented revision.
- Orders must have a traceable status history.

## 40.3 Inventory Integrity

- Prevent negative stock unless specifically authorized.
- Reserve stock for confirmed orders.
- Release reservations when orders are cancelled.
- Prevent expired contact lens products from being sold.
- Track batch and expiry data where applicable.
- Maintain stock movement history.
- Use database transactions for stock updates.

## 40.4 Laboratory Integrity

- Every lab job must link to a sales order.
- Store exact prescription and measurement snapshots.
- Track processing status.
- Record rejected and rework jobs.
- Do not mark a job complete before required checks.
- Record laboratory costs.

## 40.5 Financial Integrity

- Every payment must be traceable.
- Every refund must reference an original transaction.
- Discounts must follow permissions.
- Invoice totals must be calculated from line items.
- Tax must follow configured rules.
- Posted journal entries must balance.
- Financial records must not be silently deleted.

## 40.6 Data Integrity

- Use transactions for critical workflows.
- Prevent duplicate payment processing.
- Maintain unique document numbers.
- Keep audit history.
- Apply strict branch and company access restrictions.
